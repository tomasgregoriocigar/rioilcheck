> CURRENT STATUS

NO REFERRAL FEES. NO DEALER LISTINGS. NO PAID PLACEMENT. As of the date below, no dealer or platform pays this site anything, version 1 of this site carries no dealer listings, and nothing on this site is ranked, featured, or labelled because anyone paid for it.

> IF THAT EVER CHANGES — THE RULES, IN ADVANCE

  • Disclosure on the listing itself. If a dealer or platform ever pays a referral fee, a featured-listing fee, or any other consideration connected to a listing, that listing will say so in plain text on the listing itself — for example: “REFERRAL FEE: this dealer/platform may pay us if you order through a link on this site.” You will not have to visit this page to see it.
  • Payment never affects ranking or labels. The statewide benchmark is the EIA/SHOPP figure and cannot be influenced by anyone. Any future dealer ordering, staleness flag, or basis label will follow the verified data described in our Methodology — a dealer cannot pay to move up, remove a STALE flag, or change a basis label.
  • Paid placement will be labeled as advertising. Any future paid featured placement will be marked “ADVERTISING / PAID PLACEMENT” on its face and kept separate from any data-driven ordering.
  • No lead-selling, ever. This site does not sell, rent, or pass visitor information to dealers or platforms. See our Privacy Policy.

> WHAT A REFERRAL FEE WOULD BE

A referral fee is an amount a dealer or ordering platform pays a website when a visitor who arrived through that website's link places an order. The visitor does not pay the fee, and the dealer's price is set by the dealer — but the fee would be a financial connection between this site and that dealer, which is why it would be disclosed on the listing where a link appears, in line with the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). This page is the standing, prospective disclosure; the on-listing disclosure described above is the one that will appear if a paid relationship ever exists.
