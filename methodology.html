== METHODOLOGY & CORRECTIONS ==

> WHAT THIS SITE PUBLISHES

This site publishes the official statewide benchmark prices for Rhode Island residential heating oil and propane, explains what those averages do and do not mean, and — in a later phase, only when dealer data can be verified — may list dealer-posted prices with their own dates and basis labels. Version 1 has no dealer listings. Nothing on this site is a dealer quote, and no fuel can be ordered through this site.

> DATA SOURCE: EIA / SHOPP (FORM EIA-877)

  • The benchmark is the State Heating Oil and Propane Program (SHOPP), a joint data-collection effort of the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) and state energy offices, conducted on Form EIA-877 (the Winter Heating Fuels Telephone Survey). Rhode Island is a participating state.
  • SHOPP publishes one statewide average per fuel for Rhode Island. It does not publish county-level prices, and it does not publish dealer-level prices.
  • Price basis: the published average is the residential credit price (keep-full / automatic-delivery preferred), excluding taxes and excluding cash or prompt-pay discounts. In plain terms, it tracks the full-service, on-credit price — not the lowest cash price in the market. Cash/COD prices will often be lower; that difference is expected and is explained on the homepage, not hidden.

> CADENCE: COLLECTED MONDAY, PUBLISHED WEDNESDAY

  • In heating season (October through March), prices are collected as of Monday, submitted by state offices by Tuesday, and published by EIA on Wednesday. Every figure on this site carries its collection (Monday) date and its publication date.
  • From April through September, SHOPP collects prices only on the second Monday of each month, so the benchmark updates monthly in that period.
  • The prior week's average may be revised in the next EIA release. If a figure we published is revised, we update it and note the revision in the corrections log.
  • A Wednesday figure describes Monday-morning prices. It is roughly two days old on arrival and up to about nine days old by the following Tuesday. Prices shown are statewide averages, not dealer quotes — a dealer's price on the day you order can be higher or lower.

> HOW ANY FUTURE DEALER PRICE WILL BE LABELED

  • Every dealer figure, if dealer listings are ever added, will carry: the dealer name, the posted price, the gallon tier it applies to, a basis label (for example COD prepaid / will-call / full-service / automatic delivery), any disclosed fees, and a “verified [date]” timestamp.
  • A price past its verification window will be flagged STALE. A price that cannot be confirmed will be shown as not confirmed — never guessed.
  • No dealer will be ranked “best” on price alone. Basis differences (what the per-gallon price includes) are part of the comparison.

> PROPANE AND YOUR RIGHTS UNDER RI LAW

Rhode Island law gives propane customers specific disclosure rights (R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-83-2), including itemised disclosure of fees above the delivered cost per gallon and, on request, the estimated price per gallon and applicable charges. Content on this site describing those rights is general information only, not legal advice, and the duties described are the dealer's — this site does not enforce them. Heating oil has no equivalent RI disclosure statute, and Rhode Island does not regulate retail heating oil or propane prices.

> CORRECTIONS

  • If you believe any figure on this site is wrong or out of date, email tomas.gregorio@gmail.com with the figure, the date shown, and a source for the correct figure if you have one.
  • A challenged figure that cannot be promptly re-verified against EIA/SHOPP will be corrected or removed pending verification. Corrections are logged — we keep a dated log of each request, what was checked, and what was changed.
  • Dealers may use the same address to request a correction to any future listing about them. A correction request is handled on the verified data only.
