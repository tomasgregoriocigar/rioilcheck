== ABOUT RI OIL CHECK ==

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Operator: Gregorio Digital Companies LLC.

> WHAT THIS SITE IS

RI Oil Check tracks Rhode Island heating oil and propane prices against the official state benchmark: the U.S. Energy Information Administration's State Heating Oil and Propane Program (SHOPP) survey averages for Rhode Island. The benchmark is a survey average of the residential credit price — the top of the market's range, not a quote — and this site explains, in plain terms, what a fair price looks like against it for COD and full-service buyers.

> HOW IT MAKES MONEY

The site is supported by advertising and, where disclosed, referral fees — only ever on options this site's own comparison ranks as genuine wins for the reader. A referral fee never changes a figure shown here or what is said about it. See the Affiliate Disclosure for the current arrangements.

> HOW THE DATA IS KEPT HONEST

Benchmark figures come from EIA's published SHOPP data — collected Mondays and published Wednesdays in heating season (weekly October through March, monthly off-season) — and every figure carries its collection week. The full method is on the Methodology page. If a figure here is wrong, we want to know: see Contact.
